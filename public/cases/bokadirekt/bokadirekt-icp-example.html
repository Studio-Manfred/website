The Work-Life Balanced

ICP deep dive — challenges, drivers, business opportunity & system preferences

😤 Challenges

The primary challenge for this segment is financial security within the industry. This is closely followed by difficulty finding time for marketing to attract new clients, and balancing work with personal life.

These challenges are interconnected — insufficient time for customer acquisition directly contributes to financial insecurity and hinders work-life balance.

📈 Drivers

This group is primarily motivated by work-life balance, with a sense of flexibility and control. This is closely followed by helping other people and high customer satisfaction.

Achieving work-life balance is challenging given the time required for high customer satisfaction and marketing — both identified as major pain points.
